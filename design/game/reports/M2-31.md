## Why these choices

- **Tollkeeper-clear gate, not a level threshold.** `economy/baseline.md`'s simulation already pins
  Tollkeeper readiness to hero level 3 and shows it's won in every swept seed/spec/gear combination at
  that level — reusing the existing `BossClaim` fact as the area-2/dungeon gate is diegetic (clear the
  road's toll boss, the road beyond opens), requires no new server state, and automatically tunes area-2
  content for "a little past level 3" parties without inventing a second numeric gate.
- **One row-ignoring normal enemy before the row-ignoring boss.** Old Road has zero row-ignoring
  enemies; the Tollkeeper is everyone's first encounter with it. Giving `enemy_aqueduct_current` the
  same trait at normal-encounter stakes (lower HP/no execute mechanic) teaches the taunt counterplay
  in a cheaper, repeatable context before the dungeon boss demands it under higher stakes — this is a
  content/pacing choice, not a combat-engine change (the behavior is built entirely from `combat.md`
  8.2's existing row/taunt rules).
- **Boss cycle is 4 rounds vs. the Tollkeeper's 3, and adds an AoE beat.** The dungeon sits behind both
  the area-2 unlock and three dungeon floors, so it is deliberately tuned as a step up from the
  Tollkeeper: same "ignores row, taunt is the answer" shape, plus a periodic AoE+weaken beat
  (`Tidal Surge`) that punishes a no-healer comp independently of formation, since AoE is never
  row-gated or taunt-redirected. This produces two independent team-building levers (tank-for-taunt,
  healer-for-sustain) rather than one, raising the bar for "good team building over raw stat totals"
  as the milestone's content scope item asks.
- **No new statuses, skills, or reward-profile numbers.** Every behavior above is assembled from
  statuses and formulas `classes.md`/`combat.md` already specify (`status_weaken`, `basic()`, the
  attenuated-ATK AoE pattern Meteor/Volley already use, the taunt-override rule already shared by every
  offensive action) and every reward number comes from `gear.md`'s already-frozen `reward_area_2`/
  `reward_dungeon_1` rows. This keeps the change strictly additive content on top of frozen systems,
  per the task's explicit "do not touch combat mechanics, classes, skills, or gear/crafting rules"
  instruction.
- **`reward_area_3`/`reward_dungeon_2` left unbound.** `gear.md` states an unused profile does not
  require another area; binding only one new area and one new dungeon satisfies M2.md's "at least"
  wording for scope item 6 and acceptance check 11 without reaching beyond this task's bounded content
  scope (`AGENTS.md` rule 2 / the task's explicit "do not expand scope" instruction).
