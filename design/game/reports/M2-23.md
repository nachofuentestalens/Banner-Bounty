## Decisions, limitations and next task

- Keep early leveling and owner-approved level-3 Tollkeeper readiness. Week-long
  goals are gear/build choices; do not stretch M1 XP into an artificial grind.
- Crafting uses guaranteed dungeon materials, with bounded affix stats and gold
  sinks. A chosen replacement item allows crafting with a full bag; no automatic
  deletion, sale or salvage loop. This is a specified player action, not an action
  performed against anyone's save in this task.
- Timed dungeon floors (180 seconds), one activity at a time, terminal runs and
  atomic floor receipts prevent free instant replay farming. These new requirements
  need formalization in M2-25 and implementation in M2-26/27.
- Reward RNG remains separate from combat and client request IDs. The scoped
  precedence note resolves classes' broad no-economy-RNG wording in favor of the
  explicit owner requirement for randomized affixes and existing loot behavior.
- No encounter access time or success distribution is claimed. A daily budget with
  100% wins and a hypothetical three-floor dungeon is conditional; actual first-week
  progression must be measured against M2-31 encounters in M2-28. Existing abundant
  gold, inventory saturation and low-participation play are explicit measurement cases.

Next existing task: **M2-25** consumes the gear/material/run contract requirements;
**M2-31** binds reward profiles to its encounter content. M2-26/27 implement, M2-28
measures production distributions, M2-29 verifies observable acceptance. No new task
