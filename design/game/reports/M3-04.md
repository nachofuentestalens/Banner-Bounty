## Unresolved issues

1. **Duration-model Scenario A vs. B** (`m3.md` §2.3) is the largest open item carried to M3-05:
   whether a travel/setup floor exists alongside combat-length duration determines every minute
   band in this document by up to an order of magnitude. Not decidable by this role alone — it is
   an engineering/contract decision M3-05 owns, stated explicitly rather than guessed.
2. **Migration fixtures, offline combat-length catch-up, and guaranteed-material-failure recovery**
   (`m3.md` §3 fixtures F4/F5/F7) remain unmeasurable with the current pure-engine harness, the same
   category of gap M2-28 reported (its Finding 3) and the simulation-matrix review task partially
   closed (hero-count ledger only). Not fabricated here; the exact missing capability is named per
   fixture.
3. **The 32-paired-seed tactical protocol cannot run until M3-07 ships new content** — confirmed
   absent by direct source search (command #7 above). The protocol is fully specified and ready.
4. **Fixed-level income and actual leveling progression remain two separate, un-integrated
   sources** (`m3.md` §9) rather than one simulation that starts at level 1 and plays forward; no
   harness extension exists yet to unify them.
5. This task's own measurement runs used the pre-M3 build (six classes, two expedition areas, one
   dungeon) because that is the only build that exists; none of this evidence demonstrates anything
   about Warder, Weaver, specializations, races, or Cinder Archive — it is reused only where the
   measured mechanic (hero-count ledger accounting, catch-up timing, level-sweep contrast) is
   unchanged by M3's additions.
