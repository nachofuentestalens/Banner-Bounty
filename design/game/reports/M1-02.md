## Acceptance checks

**1. Integer math, targeting/ties, speed order, cooldowns/status durations/stacking, healing, death, victory, timeout, with worked examples.**
- Check: every formula in `combat.md` uses only `+`, `-`, `*`, and explicit `floor(...)` integer division (sections 1.2, 3.4-3.7, 4); no stat uses a bare `/`.
- Check: `combat.md` section 2 fixes round structure (speed sort → actions → status ticks → duration decrement → resolution check) and section 3.1 fixes all three targeting rules (enemy-vs-hero by position, hero-vs-enemy by lowest HP, hero-vs-ally by lowest HP%) with explicit tie-breaks.
- Check: section 3.3 states explicitly that M1 has no cooldowns and why (automatic abilities re-evaluate every turn). Section 3.7 fixes Burn's 2-stack cap, refresh-on-reapply, and simultaneous dual-stack expiry.
- Check: section 4/3.6 fixes the healing formula and overheal-discard cap. Section 5 fixes death (immediate removal from targeting/speed/ticks), victory (checked post-round, never mid-round), simultaneous-defeat (loss in PvE/boss, draw in arena — not reachable by the content tables as given, but the branch is specified), and 100-round timeout (loss in PvE/boss, draw in arena, matching `first-playable.md`).
- Result: **pass**. Section 6 gives 11 worked examples, one per rule area, including a full 7-round hand trace (example 11) proving a level-1, unequipped, unspecialized party clears the three Old Road enemies inside the round cap — independently re-derived and checked by hand during this task (brigand/wolf/cutpurse totals and the level-1 class stats from `content.md`/`combat.md` section 1.1).

**2. Each specialization changes an observable outcome; automatic ability behavior and unlock/switch rules defined.**
- Check: all 6 specs (`combat.md` 3.4-3.6) override either damage output (Berserker's +40%/-20%/execute-adjacent HP-threshold bonus, Marksman's execute-threshold +50%), survivability (Guardian's redirect + flat DEF, Warden's healing), or turn order (Pathfinder's party-wide +2 SPD), and Pyromancer adds a new damage channel (Burn DOT) — each is a numerically distinct, observable change from the class default action, not a cosmetic relabel.
- Check: unlock (`level >= 3`, matching `contracts.md`'s `/hero/specialization` validation) and free outside-expedition switching (blocked mid-expedition by `active_expedition`, consistent with `contracts.md` worked examples 9-10) are both stated in section 3.3, with the frozen-vs-live distinction for expedition cycles vs. boss/arena challenges made explicit.
- Check: no spec is mandatory — section 3.3 and worked example 11 demonstrate a fully unspecialized party clears Old Road content, satisfying `first-playable.md`'s "no single mandatory specialization" requirement.
- Result: **pass**.

**3. Content tables: IDs, stats, slot restrictions, enemy compositions, boss behavior, no runtime-ambiguous placeholders.**
- Check: `content.md` section 2 gives 9 stable `definitionId`s (exactly 3 per slot, meeting the "at least 9" floor) with integer HP/ATK/DEF bonuses and a hard slot restriction per `contracts.md`'s equip validation; section 2.2 gives exactly 3 `affixId`s with single-stat flat bonuses; section 2.3 gives a fully specified comparison/sort rule (no "TBD" sort behavior).
- Check: section 3 gives 3 enemy definitions with fixed stats and a fixed, deterministic per-cycle composition (no randomized-selection algorithm left unspecified, since M1 deliberately never varies the composition — recorded as a scoped simplification, not a gap).
- Check: section 4 gives the Tollkeeper's stats and a fully deterministic, round-number-keyed behavior pattern (Toll Smash every 3rd round, Toll Strike otherwise) — no RNG-dependent or player-choice-dependent branch.
- Result: **pass**. No field in either table is left as a placeholder; every ID, stat, and restriction has a concrete value.

**4. Align battle/reset/loadout rules with M1-01; leave reward values to the economy table.**

## Limitations

- Enemy and boss stats are fixed, not scaled to hero level — a deliberate M1 simplification recorded in `combat.md` 1.3 and `content.md` section 3; a future difficulty-tier system is out of scope here.
- The full numeric balance across all 10 levels and both specialization choices per class is not exhaustively hand-traced (only the unspecialized level-1 case and isolated single-mechanic examples are traced); broader pacing validation (first-boss-clear timing, per-level difficulty curve) is explicitly deferred to M1-04's economy tuning and M1-12's simulation benchmarks, consistent with `first-playable.md`'s framing of pacing targets as hypotheses to measure, not guarantees this document can certify alone.
- The simultaneous-both-sides-defeated branch (section 5) is specified for completeness but not reachable by the exact stat tables in `content.md` as given; it is not exercised by any worked example, only asserted by rule.
