# M2: Deepen the loop and close M1's flagged gaps

## Goal

M1 proved the prepare/dispatch/collect/improve loop works end to end and is accepted, but
`design/studio/reports/M1-16.md` and `qa/reports/M1-15.md` flag real open items: a thin boss
combat margin, and four acceptance areas that were never independently exercised (boss-loss
recovery, the 100-item inventory-overflow boundary, malformed-payload handling, and
accessibility contrast). Vision.md frames M1 as proof of "a company worth returning to" — the
next most valuable step is to give the player a reason to keep returning (a second expedition
area/content tier) while rebalancing the boss fight, and to close the specific coverage gaps the
studio itself flagged rather than letting them linger untested indefinitely.

M2 scope: add one new expedition area (new normal-enemy roster, reusing the existing boss or a
light variant — no new class/spec/account features), rebalance the existing boss's thin margin,
and independently exercise the four flagged M1 gaps. No shops, crafting, matchmaking, guilds,
chat, monetization, mobile/desktop packaging, or procedural campaign — same exclusions as M1.

## Done means

- The new area is implementation-complete and playable through real server endpoints and the
  browser UI, with no regression to M1's area, boss, saves, sessions, or arena behavior.
- The boss margin finding from M1-16 is addressed with simulation evidence, without breaking the
  on-target level-3 specialization-unlock pacing from `design/game/economy/tuning.md`.
- All four gaps flagged in M1-15/M1-16 (boss-loss recovery, inventory-overflow boundary,
  malformed-payload handling, accessibility contrast) have been independently exercised with
  concrete pass/fail evidence — not re-asserted from memory.
- No open P0/P1 defect (save loss, reward duplication, account-isolation break, broken acceptance
  gate, missing core action).
- QA Tester independently verifies the full set of M2 acceptance checks below, and the Game
  Director records a milestone verdict referencing that evidence.

## Acceptance checks

1. Second expedition area (new enemy roster, stable content IDs) is specified, content-validated
   and implemented; area 1's enemies, items and boss remain unchanged and unregressed.
2. The boss-margin rebalance from M1-16's "thin margin" finding has fresh simulation evidence and
   does not materially shift the already-on-target level-3 specialization-unlock pacing documented
   in `design/game/economy/tuning.md`.
3. New area/enemy copy is original, keyed to the new content IDs, and consistent with
   `design/game/narrative/copy.md`'s existing tone; no copied text.
4. A player can select, run, and collect from the new area end to end through real persisted
   server state and the browser UI; no hard-coded success or disconnected placeholder.
5. Boss-loss recovery is independently exercised (a forced loss, the UI/API retry path, no state
   corruption) with pass/fail evidence — closes the M1-16 gap.
6. The 100-item inventory-overflow boundary (cap behavior, gold conversion, no equipped-item loss)
   is independently exercised with pass/fail evidence — closes the M1-15 gap.
7. Malformed/oversized request payloads against the Zod validation boundary are independently
   exercised with pass/fail evidence — closes the M1-15 gap.
8. An accessibility contrast-ratio audit of the core screens (Expedition, Inventory, Company,
   Boss, Arena) is performed; any defect found is fixed or logged as a concrete P2 — closes the
   M1-16 gap.
9. Fresh `npm ci`, `npm run validate`, and a full browser walkthrough pass on the integrated
   release candidate; no new P0/P1 defect is introduced by the new area or the rebalance.
10. The Game Director's verdict references checks 1-9 with concrete evidence, states what remains
    untested (if anything), and gives the owner a short playtest script for the new content.
