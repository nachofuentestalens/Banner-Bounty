# M2: watchable combat and Idle Adventure-style depth

Owner direction (2026-10-20), replacing the earlier "second area + boss rebalance" M2 plan after
the owner's M1 playtest verdict: "way too basic, doesn't look at all like Idle Adventure, the
fights are extremely boring. I need to be able to see what each unit is doing, how much HP they
have left." This document is the frozen scope for the M2 task graph (M2-21..M2-30). Earlier M2
work that is still valid and integrated: M2-01 (area-2 contract amendment, additive only — see
`design/studio/reports/M2-01.md`), M2-07 (gap tests) and M2-08 (contrast audit, see
`reports/engineering/M2-08.md`).

Idle Adventure (https://store.steampowered.com/app/714020/Idle_Adventure/) is a genre reference
for depth and feel only. Never copy its names, text, or assets.

## Goal

Make combat watchable and tactically interesting, and give the hero/class/gear system the depth
the owner expects from an Idle-Adventure-like game, while keeping M1's architecture: mostly text
UI, deterministic server-authoritative combat, offline progression, async arena, local hosting
only.

## Scope

1. **Watchable, controllable combat.** The battle view shows every unit on both sides: name,
   class, HP bar with exact numbers, and active status effects. A turn-by-turn feed states who
   acted, which skill they used, on which target, the exact damage or healing number, and who
   died. Playback controls — pause, step, speed, skip — work the same way across expeditions,
   dungeons, bosses, and arena replays.
2. **Combat v2 with real decisions.** Skills with cooldowns, buffs/debuffs, taunt/threat,
   healing, single-target and AoE effects, crit/dodge rolls, and front/back row targeting rules.
   Combat stays deterministic and server-authoritative (same seed/log → same replay).
3. **Class roster and dual-class heroes.** At least 6 base classes spanning tank, healer,
   physical damage, magic damage, and support. Each hero combines two classes (primary +
   secondary) with documented synergies between specific pairings. Each hero has a skill loadout
   (active and passive skills chosen from the unlocked skills of its two classes).
4. **Roster and formation.** A recruitable hero roster larger than the active party; a party of
   up to 5 heroes with an explicit front/back formation that matters for targeting and threat.
5. **Gear depth.** Rarity tiers, randomized affixes, and crafting/reforging using materials found
   in dungeons.
6. **Content.** Several expedition areas (building on the M2-01 area contract) and multi-floor
   dungeons with bosses, tuned so difficulty rewards good team building (class synergy, formation,
   gear) over raw stat totals.

## Out of scope

No live cooperative combat, matchmaking beyond the existing async arena snapshot model, shared
market, guilds, chat, monetization, procedural campaign, mobile app, Steam integration, desktop
packaging, generated art campaign, public account system, or hosted/cloud infrastructure. No
real-time rendered graphics — the battle view is the richer text/structured-data UI described
above, not a graphical combat renderer. No new multiplayer account model beyond M1's two-local-
account pattern.

## Done means

A fresh local install can run the M2 acceptance walkthrough (`design/qa/acceptance.md`, extended
for M2 by M2-29's evidence) and:

- Build a 5-hero party from a larger roster, each hero with a primary+secondary class and a
  chosen skill loadout, placed in a front/back formation.
- Watch an expedition, a dungeon run, a dungeon boss fight, and an arena replay in the same
  battle view, each showing live HP, status effects, and a readable turn-by-turn action feed, with
  working pause/step/speed/skip.
- See combat decisions matter: a poorly built team (no healer, no tank, wrong formation) loses or
  struggles against content a well-built team of the same average power clears.
- Find dungeon materials, craft or reforge a gear affix, and see the resulting stat change reflected
  in the next fight.
- Hit no P0/P1 defect (startup failure, save loss/corruption, cross-account mutation, duplicate
  reward, inaccessible core action, or a battle view that cannot show what happened in a fight).

QA certifies the acceptance checks below with real commands/evidence; the Game Director records
the final playability verdict (M2-30).

## Acceptance checks (QA Tester verifies in the browser)

1. **Roster and recruitment.** Recruit heroes into a roster strictly larger than the max party
   size (5); confirm the roster screen lists more heroes than can be fielded at once.
2. **Dual class and skill loadout.** Open a hero's build screen; assign a primary and a secondary
   class from the ≥6-class roster; choose an active and a passive skill from each class's unlocked
   skill list; confirm the loadout persists after navigating away and back (and after a server
   restart).
3. **Formation.** Arrange a 5-hero party into front and back rows; confirm the saved formation
   persists and is reflected in the battle view's unit layout.
4. **Battle view completeness.** Start any fight (expedition, dungeon, boss, or arena) and confirm
   the view shows, for every unit on both sides: name, class, a numeric HP bar, and current
   status effects (e.g., a buff/debuff icon or text with a visible duration).
5. **Turn-by-turn feed.** Confirm the action feed logs, for each turn, the actor, the skill used,
   the target(s), the exact damage/healing number, and an explicit death notice when a unit's HP
   reaches 0.
6. **Playback controls.** During a fight, use pause, step (advance exactly one action), change
   speed, and skip-to-end; confirm each control produces the expected, correct battle state (no
   desync between the control used and the HP/log shown).
7. **Tactical decisions have a measurable effect.** Run the same encounter with two different
   valid team builds (e.g., with and without a healer, or two different formations) and confirm
   the outcome (clear/fail, or turns-to-clear, or HP remaining) differs in the direction the build
   change predicts.
8. **Cooldowns, buffs/debuffs, and taunt/threat are visible and enforced.** Confirm a skill on
   cooldown cannot be reused before it's ready, confirm an active buff/debuff changes a stat as
   stated, and confirm a taunt/high-threat unit draws enemy single-target attacks over lower-
   threat allies.
9. **Gear rarity and affixes.** Confirm equipped gear shows a rarity tier and randomized affixes,
   and that the affix values differ between two drops of the same item at the same rarity.
10. **Crafting and reforging.** Collect dungeon materials, craft or reforge one piece of gear, and
    confirm the result consumes the stated materials and changes the item's affix(es) or tier.
11. **Multiple areas and multi-floor dungeons with bosses.** Confirm at least two expedition areas
    are selectable (per the M2-01 contract) and at least one dungeon has multiple floors ending in
    a boss fight, with the boss fight using the same full battle view as other fights.
12. **No regression on M1 core loop.** Confirm prepare → dispatch → collect → improve still works
    end to end for the simplest case (single hero, single expedition) without a P0/P1 defect, and
    that the M2-08 contrast fixes and M2-01 area contract remain in effect.
