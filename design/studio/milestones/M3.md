Date: 2026-10-29. Owner: Game Director. Status: scoped for Producer task creation; not implemented.

## Player promise and boundaries

Watch successive fights reliably, understand and revise a build, then test a distinctive company
in dungeons and bounded asynchronous competition. Expand meaningful choices before adding volume.
Keep deterministic pure game rules, one authoritative local server/SQLite, original text, existing
accessible structured battle view, and five-hero maximum. Level cap stays 10. No paid service,
public deployment/accounts, live co-op, real-time PvP, guilds, chat, market or new renderer.

Team PvP means one account's up-to-five-hero company against another account's immutable company
snapshot. It does not mean several humans controlling one team. Two local human test accounts
remain sufficient; server-owned fixture opponents fill a small tournament bracket, clearly labelled
as fixtures. Never silently invent human players. This is a local prototype of seasons/tournaments,
not an online population or matchmaking service.

## Evidence and economy policy

Preserve the owner's early level-3 boss readiness; do not restore an arbitrary 20–40 minute grind.
Economy first specifies provisional measured-minute bands, then evaluates production simulations
before accepting tuned values. Cover 1/3/5 heroes, fresh and migrated saves, 30/84/480 credited
minutes/day, purchase/respec costs, overflow, failure recovery and offline partitions. The 84-minute
case reflects the corrected four-floor M2 budget. Fixed-level income alone is insufficient.

For tactical contrast, use at least 32 paired seeds per selected encounter/build pair with levels,
gear budget, initial stats, skills and formation recorded. Report clear rate, turns and normalized
HP/survivors; include counterexamples. No requirement that healer/tank is universally best. Broaden
to all eight primary classes and new specialization/race effects so a single best pairing is not
mistaken for depth. Do not change rewards to disguise a combat-balance problem.
