## One technical decision recorded (reversible, within documented CTO authority)

`economy/m3.md` §2.3 left open whether `nominalDurationMs` carries a wall-clock floor in addition to
`resolvedRounds * msPerRound` ("Scenario A" vs "Scenario B"), and explicitly deferred that decision to
this task ("Required before freezing either scenario (M3-05)... M3-05 must decide whether a floor
exists alongside combat-length duration," `m3-systems.md` §3/§12). **Decision: no floor (Scenario A).**
Rationale recorded in `contracts.md` §0: `m3-systems.md`'s own frozen formula has no floor term, and
adding one here would contradict the already-frozen, higher-precedence Design document rather than
implement it; the milestone text assigns this exact class of decision to CTO. The consequence —
`baseline.md`/`tuning.md`'s minute-band text will read wrong in wall-clock terms once this ships,
though cycle-*count* milestones (12 cycles to level 3, etc.) are unaffected — is stated explicitly
rather than hidden, with the required Economy follow-up (re-derive minute bands once M3-07 ships real
content, already anticipated by `economy/m3.md` §10 item 1 and the milestone's M3-10 row) named as a
non-blocking next step, not silently left unresolved. This was not escalated as a question to the owner
because the governing documents already delegate it to CTO and it is reversible (a later rules-version
bump can reintroduce a floor without a shape change, per `contracts.md` §1's versioned-constant design).
